    Method & limitations

    • Data: a documented, permitted job-posting dataset; source, access date and license recorded. Prototype uses sample data only.
    • Salary target: valid salary records only, normalized to INR per annum.
    • Models: regression pipeline with ColumnTransformer, trained on the training split only, evaluated on a held-out set with cross-validation.
    • Resume NLP: curated skill dictionary and alias matching; results are user-reviewable.
    • Limits: postings may be biased or outdated; disclosed salaries don’t represent every employee; no sensitive personal attributes are used.
    • Privacy: resumes are processed transiently and never stored in this prototype.
